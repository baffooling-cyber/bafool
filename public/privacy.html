RUN THE TABLE

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated November 15, 2026

WHAT WE COLLECT

You can play Run the Table without an account. Your game history is then kept only in your own browser.

If you make an account, we store:

  • Your display name.
  • For email sign-in: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your actual password.
  • For Google, Apple or Discord sign-in: the account ID that service gives us, plus the name and email it shares (Discord shares only your username).
  • Your game stats: seasons, records, Perfect Run progress, rings, daily results, fantasy seasons and Run the Grid games.

If you post a score to the global leaderboard, the name and score you post are shown publicly.

HOW WE USE IT

Only to sign you in, keep your stats in sync across devices, and show the leaderboard. We do not sell your account data or share it with anyone else. A single sign-in cookie keeps you logged in.

ADVERTISING

We show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

WHERE IT'S STORED

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Netlify.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Open Your Account on the site and choose Delete my account. That permanently removes your account and saved stats. Scores you already posted to the leaderboard stay there.
