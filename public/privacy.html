Run the Table

Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

What we collect

You can play Run the Table without an account. Your game history is then kept only in your own browser.

If you make an account, we store:

  • Your display name.
  • For email sign-in: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your actual password.
  • For Google, Apple or Discord sign-in: the account ID that service gives us, plus the name and email it shares (Discord shares only your username).
  • Your game stats: seasons, records, Perfect Run progress, rings, daily results, fantasy seasons and Run the Grid games.

If you post a score to the global leaderboard, the name and score you post are shown publicly.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, keep your stats in sync across devices, and show the leaderboard. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else. A single sign-in cookie keeps you logged in.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Netlify.

Deleting your data

Open Your Account on the site and choose Delete my account. That permanently removes your account and saved stats. Scores you already posted to the leaderboard stay there.
